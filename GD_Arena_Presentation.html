THE REAL CHALLENGE

Why Students Fail Group Discussions

PROBLEM 01

The Solitary Mirage

Practicing in a Vacuum

Students practice in front of a mirror or generic 1-on-1 chatbots where nobody interrupts, challenges, or cross-questions.

  • Real placement rounds throw 6–8 candidates into chaotic debates.
  • Zero preparation for cut-ins, conversational collisions, or timing openings.
  • Panic and speech freezes occur during the crucial first 30 seconds.
PROBLEM 02

Imbalanced Dynamics

Loudest Voices Monopolize

In standard peer groups, 1 or 2 aggressive speakers capture 70%+ of airtime, leaving quieter candidates with zero opportunity.

  • No trained neutral facilitator exists in hostel or college study groups.
  • Valuable insights from introverted students are completely drowned out.
  • Students don't learn diplomatic de-escalation or inclusive handoffs.
PROBLEM 03

Subjective Feedback

Vague & Unactionable

Traditional feedback amounts to generic advice like "sound more confident" or "speak louder".

  • No objective data on speaking pace (Words Per Minute) or talk time share.
  • No audit of crutch filler words ("basically", "like", "actually") in context.
  • No quote-level verification of whether an argument was logical or flawed.
THE SOLUTION

Realistic Multi-Persona Simulation

CORE MECHANISM

3 AI Personas + 1 Student

True Group Dynamic Simulation

  • AI Moderator (Rahul / Priya): Regulates time, sets agenda, ensures mutual decorum, and intervenes neutrally when participants collide.
  • Supportive Peer (Ananya): Reinforces productive arguments, introduces industry statistics, and models constructive agreement.
  • Skeptical Challenger (Vikram): Interrogates unsupported claims, challenges weak reasoning, and tests composure under pressure.
  • Structured Progression: Enforces standard 3-phase GD lifecycle (Opening Stances → Cross-Rebuttals → Synthesis & Conclusion).
AUDIO & INTERACTION

Natural Speech Experience

Seamless Dual-Mode Conversations

  • Full Speech-to-Speech Flow: Browser-native Web Speech API STT (Speech-to-Text) + Neural TTS (Text-to-Speech) with zero backend audio bandwidth.
  • Bilingual Indian Context: Native code-switching in English and Hinglish ("Suniyega, Vikram's point has data, but ground reality is different...").
  • Push-to-Speak & Live Collision Detection: Students can assertively interject or hold to speak, replicating real corporate interview pressure.
  • Live Audio Visualizers: Pulsing soundwave rings and active speaker cards keep participants engaged.
KEY INNOVATION

Smart Participation Balancer

METRIC 01

Fair Opportunity Index (FOI)

Continuous mathematical equity metric calculating talk time share, turn frequency, and word count distribution across the table in real time.

SAFETY 02

Dominance Detection & Badges

Automated warning threshold triggers if any speaker crosses >40% total airtime, flagging monopolization visually and guiding the room back to equilibrium.

MODERATION 03

Diplomatic Moderator Prompts

The AI Moderator actively detects quieter speakers and invites them diplomatically: "Rudra, what is your perspective on this aspect?"

ASSIST 04

Suggested Entry Chips

Interactive 1-click starter chips appear on the student screen (e.g. "Building on that point...", "A differing perspective...") so quiet students can jump in seamlessly.

EVALUATION ENGINE

Deterministic Data + AI Evaluation

MATHEMATICAL ENGINE

Deterministic Python Metrics

Calculated From Exact Timestamps

  • Talk Time Share %: Exact millisecond speaking duration tallied per participant.
  • Speaking Rate (WPM): Precise words-per-minute tokenized from audio transcript to diagnose rushing vs sluggish delivery.
  • Filler-Word Frequency: Regex audit for crutch patterns ("basically", "like", "actually", "um") paired with surrounding sentence context.
  • Interruption Latency: Quantifies response delay and conversational hesitation between turns.
AI INSIGHTS

Actionable Coaching Playbook

Zero Hallucinations Guarantee

  • Verified Transcript Citations: Every critique links directly to an exact spoken sentence chip from the discussion.
  • 4-Pillar Competency Scoring: 1-to-10 breakdown for Argument Logic, Handling Counter-Arguments, Active Listening, and Structure.
  • Comparative Peer Ratings: Compares student benchmark alongside AI peers so candidates see where they stand.
  • 3-Step Next GD Playbook: Practical, high-yield drill steps tailored to the student's exact weaknesses.
